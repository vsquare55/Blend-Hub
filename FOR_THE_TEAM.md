# Blend Hub — How to sign in and use it

*You don't need to install anything. Just a web browser. Works on your computer and your phone.*

---

## What you were given

- **A link**, like `https://something.trycloudflare.com`
- **A username** (e.g. `rahul`)
- **A first password**

Keep these private. The link + your login is how you get in.

---

## Sign in — on a computer (Windows / Mac)

1. Open **Edge, Chrome, or Safari**.
2. Go to the **link** you were given.
3. Type your **username** and **password**, click **Sign in**.
4. Change your password: top-right → **Password** (do this the first time).

## Sign in — on iPhone / iPad

1. Open **Safari**.
2. Go to the **link**.
3. Sign in with your username and password.
4. **Tip — make it feel like an app:** tap the **Share** button (box with an arrow) →
   **Add to Home Screen**. Now "Blend Hub" sits on your home screen like a normal app.

---

## What you can do depends on your access level (shown top-right)

| Your badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | You can **see and read** everything, and print/export. You can't change data. |
| **Editor** | You can **add and change** deals, comments, needs and actions, and generate decks. |
| **Master** | Full control, including managing who can sign in. |

If you need to change something but only see **Viewer**, ask whoever runs the Hub to make you an Editor.

---

## Everyday things to know

- **Everyone works at the same time.** When a colleague changes something, your screen updates on its own
  within a few seconds — no need to refresh.
- **There is no "Save" button for most things.** Changes are saved the moment you make them.
- **Every change is logged** with your name and the time, so the team can see who did what.
- **Made a mistake?** Use **Undo** (or Ctrl/⌘ + Z) for your last change. Deleted comments/actions can be
  restored from a project's **History**.
- **Sign out** when you're done on a shared or public device: top-right → **Sign out**.

---

## If something's wrong

- **"This site can't be reached"** → the Hub host computer may be off or asleep. Tell the person who runs it.
- **The link doesn't work anymore** → the link can occasionally change; ask for the current one.
- **Forgot your password** → ask the Hub's master to reset it for you.

---

*That's everything. Open the link, sign in, and you're in.*
